About me

My name is Zuzana. I am a woman, a mother, and a guide on the journey to deep self-discovery and true inner fulfillment.

I love yoga, dance, nature, and beauty of all kinds. I am fascinated by the depths of the human psyche and the spiritual dimensions of our being, and I seek ways to connect them with the material world so that they support each other and create a harmonious whole.

Around the time my second son was born, I began to go through a profound awakening and my own inner transformation. During the following years, I experienced burnout, the breakdown of my marriage, and the collapse of my life as I had known it until then. I was forced to feel a multitude of heavy emotions that I had previously avoided and to re-evaluate my approach to myself and to life as such. I had to admit to myself where I hadn't truly been living in alignment with my truth. I had to start accepting all my long-suppressed parts, trying to understand and integrate them.

During this process of mine, I sought help and tried various methods of inner healing, self-development, conscious manifestation... The Journey method proved to be the most effective and comprehensive for me. Thanks to it, I became more clearly aware of and processed traumas from my childhood, as well as from the deeper past of my soul. I understood many karmic connections. I discovered negative beliefs that had been subtly influencing my reality without me even realizing it. I came to understand that my old life fell apart because it was not in alignment with who I truly am.

I began to perceive and understand my body, my heart, and my mind more intensely. I connected more firmly with my inner guidance. It became clear to me how I had been losing this connection, and also how to regain it—lovingly, with understanding, acceptance, and forgiveness. I changed the approach to my yoga practice. And perhaps the most beautiful thing of all—I learned to experience myself in my purest form, as an inseparable part of the Source of this life—of infinite energy, love, and consciousness.

About The Journey Method

The Journey Method was developed by world-renowned therapist, teacher, and author Brandon Bays. It was created based on her own life experience, during which she managed to heal a basketball-sized tumor within 7 weeks. She describes her story in her book The Journey. It is a simple yet highly effective technique based on releasing emotional wounds and triggering the body's self-healing process.

It is scientifically proven that emotions trigger biochemical reactions in our bodies. When emotions are suppressed, cell receptors become blocked, disrupting healthy communication between cells. This creates a disorder in the body that is initially hidden but, over time, can develop into a disease at the exact site of these blocked cell receptors. A large portion of these blockages originates in early childhood, manifesting both as a predisposition to certain health problems and as negative psychological programming. From unprocessed traumatic experiences, we carry away conclusions and beliefs that then block the natural flow of our lives and the fulfillment of our potential.

During a session, suppressed emotions are fully re-experienced and released, uncovering the related cellular memories that cause the client's difficulties. These memories are processed to bring about inner reconciliation and forgiveness. At the same time, the client finds a path to a deep inner experience of peace, unity, love, and fulfillment.

My offer

I offer my clients, above all, a safe space where they can relax, trust, and open up to an inner process of discovery and transformation. I use the Journey Method and draw upon my lifelong yoga practice.

What I will help you achieve:

  • consciously and lovingly dive into the depths of your being
  • uncover memories of life events that caused distortions and understand their meaning
  • release blocked emotions and process traumatic events to find resolution and peace
  • eliminate limiting beliefs formed during those experiences and establish new, empowering ones
  • treat yourself to an experience of deep relaxation and fulfillment by immersing yourself in your inner source
  • uncover the psychological root causes of health issues
  • gradually become aware of the structure of your personality (ego) and transcend its boundaries in both spiritual and practical life
